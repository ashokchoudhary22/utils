1. Introduction

Gammawill ("we," "our," or "us") provides OnTrack: Habit Tracker ("the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

OnTrack helps users create and manage habits, track completion history, and schedule reminders.

2. Information Stored by the App

OnTrack stores information you enter or create while using its habit-tracking features, which may include:

  • Habit names and descriptions.
  • Habit emojis and schedules.
  • Reminder preferences and times.
  • Habit completion status and history.

This information is stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account to use its habit-tracking features.

3. How Information Is Used

The information stored by the app is used to provide its core features, including:

  • Creating, viewing, editing, and deleting habits.
  • Recording and displaying habit completion history.
  • Managing habit schedules and reminder preferences.
  • Scheduling and displaying habit reminders.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

OnTrack is designed with privacy in mind. Your habit information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's habit-tracking and reminder features.

We do not sell your personal information or share your habit information with third parties for advertising purposes. OnTrack does not require an account to use its core features.

5. Notifications and Permissions

The app uses Android notification and alarm functionality to provide habit reminders. Depending on your settings and reminder configuration, notifications may display habit-related information.

You can manage notification permissions and lock-screen notification visibility through your device settings. Exact-alarm functionality may also depend on Android permissions and system settings.

6. Data Storage and Backups

Habit information is stored locally on your device. The app's backup settings are configured to exclude app data from supported Android cloud-backup and device-transfer operations.

Backup and transfer behavior may vary depending on your Android version, device manufacturer, and system configuration.

Your habits and completion history remain available while the app's local data is retained. If you uninstall the app, clear its storage, or change devices, your locally stored information may not be recoverable.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your habit information remains in the app's local storage until you delete it or remove the app's local data.

You can delete individual habits using the app's deletion feature. Deleting a habit also deletes its associated completion history.

You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall it through Android settings. These actions may permanently remove locally stored information.

8. Children's Privacy

OnTrack is a general-purpose habit-tracking application. The app does not require an account to use its habit-tracking features, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account system.

If you have questions about children's privacy or the app's privacy practices, you can contact us using the details in Section 11.

9. Security

Habit information is stored in the app's local storage on your device. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date and using appropriate device security settings to help protect your information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published at the privacy-policy URL with a revised effective date.
